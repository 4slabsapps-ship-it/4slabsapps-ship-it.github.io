TagPrice: Privacy policy

TagPrice: US Sales Tax, by 4slab. In force from 22 October 2026.

What the app keeps

TagPrice adds US sales tax to the price on a shelf tag and can show the total in a second currency. Everything you enter (prices, your cart, receipts saved to the trip ledger, a trip budget, the places you chose and your settings) is stored on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours that receives it.

Location

If you allow it, the app asks the phone for an approximate location and uses it, on the phone, to find which ZIP code you are in and therefore which tax rate applies. The location is never sent anywhere and is not stored beyond the ZIP code it led to. You can type a ZIP code or a city instead and never grant the permission.

Camera

The price-tag scanner reads the frame on the phone to find a price. No photo is taken, saved or sent. On Android the text is read by Google's ML Kit library, which runs on the phone; ML Kit sends Google its own usage and performance metrics (for example the device model, the app version, how long a read took and error codes), never the picture or the text it read. On iPhone the text is read by Apple's Vision framework, on the phone, and nothing is sent.

What goes over the network

  • The exchange rate, only if you have chosen a second currency: at most one request a day to the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.dev), which republishes the European Central Bank's reference rates, and at most one a week for the list of currencies.
  • A check for new tax data, at most once a day.

These requests carry no identifier, no location and nothing you entered. Like any request on the internet they reveal your IP address to the server that answers them.

Purchases

The one optional purchase, TagPrice Pro, is handled entirely by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. TagPrice learns only whether the purchase is owned; it never sees your payment details. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies govern the transaction.

Notifications

With Pro, the app can remind you before a state's tax-free days. The reminder is scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent to us.

Backups

Android and iOS can include the app's data in the phone's own backup to your Google or Apple account, under the terms of that service. TagPrice does not control that backup and cannot read it.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app shows no advertising and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software of its own. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
